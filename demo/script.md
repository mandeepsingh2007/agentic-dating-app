# Demo video script (3:00)

**0:00 - 0:05**  This is Date by Proxy. Every person is an AI agent, and the agents date each other.

**0:05 - 0:17**  Let's add a real person, live. I paste Ashneer Grover's LinkedIn and his public Instagram, say he is a man looking for women, and hit create.

**0:17 - 0:30**  Apify is now scraping both profiles for real. The LinkedIn scraper pulls his experience, education and skills. The Instagram scraper pulls his bio and latest posts.

**0:30 - 0:42**  Now the Analyst agent reads everything. A vision model describes his Instagram photos, and a reasoning model writes his needs, hobbies and values.

**0:42 - 0:59**  His agent is now on real dates with every woman in the pool. Each agent only knows its own person, and every message is a separate model call. You can watch the conversation live.

**0:59 - 1:06**  After each date, both agents write a private verdict. Here is his ranking.

**1:06 - 1:34**  This is his profile page. Who he is, what his Instagram shows, what he needs in a partner, his hobbies, interests, values and dealbreakers. Tap any tag to see the exact LinkedIn field or Instagram post behind it, with a confidence score. On the right, who fits him best.

**1:34 - 1:59**  Let's open his best date. Two agents, eight turns: icebreaker, lifestyle, values and goals, dealbreakers, and a closing. Then each side writes a private verdict: fit, chemistry, values, lifestyle, second date or not, and the reasons, quoting the conversation.

**1:59 - 2:09**  Date Night shows every date in the pool. Thirty real people and nearly two hundred dates, all really run.

**2:09 - 2:28**  Rankings combine both verdicts: seventy percent your agent's view, thirty percent theirs, a penalty for a dealbreaker, and a bonus when both want a second date. Here are the strongest mutual matches and everyone's top five.

**2:28 - 2:44**  How it works: Apify scrapes only LinkedIn and public Instagram, Groq runs the agents, Supabase stores every message and verdict, and dates only happen between people who would want to meet.

**2:44 - 3:01**  Thirty real people, every insight backed by evidence. Paste your own links on the live site, and your agent goes on its first dates.
